Pomodoro Timer to Increase Your Productivity

What is Pomodoro.uz?

Pomodoro.uz is an app inspired by the Pomodoro Technique, a time management method developed by Francesco Cirillo. The goal of this app is to help you focus fully on any task, whether it's reading, writing, or programming.

What is the Pomodoro Technique?

The Pomodoro Technique was created by Francesco Cirillo as a more efficient way to work and study. This technique uses a timer to divide work into intervals, usually 25 minutes long, separated by short breaks. Each interval of work is called a "pomodoro." The word means "tomato" in Italian, and is named after the tomato-shaped kitchen timer that Cirillo used when he was a university student.

25 Minutes of Pure Focus: Start the timer and lock in. No distractions — just you and the goal!

5-Minute Reward: When the timer rings, stop immediately. Stretch, grab some water, recharge your brain.

Long Break After 4 Cycles (15–30 min): Completed 4 rounds? Take a well-deserved extended break!

The Result: Great achievements are built on small 25-minute wins. Start right now!
